In short

PhotoLens does not collect any data about you. There is no PhotoLens account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and we run no servers that the app talks to. Your photos and videos travel from your device to the NAS you have chosen, and nowhere else.

This policy applies to PhotoLens for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV. Last updated 11 November 2026.

What the app handles, and where it goes

Your photos and videos

When you turn on backup, PhotoLens reads the Photos library on your iPhone, iPad or Mac and copies the photos and videos to the shared folder on the NAS you have connected. On a Mac you can also import photos and videos from files and folders you choose; they are copied to the NAS in the same way. When you browse, it reads photos and videos from that NAS. The transfer goes directly between your device and your NAS over your own network or VPN. We never receive your photos, their file names or their metadata such as dates and locations.

PhotoLens only reads the Photos library. It never changes or deletes anything in it.

Your NAS connection

The address, share name, user name and password of your NAS are stored in the Keychain on your device. So that your other devices can connect without typing them again, they are also saved in your iCloud Keychain and in iCloud key-value storage, in your own iCloud account. Apple stores that data on your behalf under Apple's privacy policy. We cannot read it.

Data kept on the device

The app keeps thumbnails, parts of videos you have watched and its settings on the device, to make browsing fast. You can remove the cache in Settings › Storage › Clear Cache, and everything is removed when you delete the app.

The demo library

Explore Demo Library uses sample photos that the app generates and stores on the device. It needs no network connection and no account. It reads your own photos only if you tap Try Backup there, on iPhone or iPad: the app then asks for access to Photos and copies your 12 most recent photos and videos into the demo library. The copies stay on the device and are deleted when you leave the demo library. On a Mac, files you import while in the demo library are copied into it and stay on the Mac until you delete them there or remove the app.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Photos, to back up your photos and videos to your NAS. Asked when you turn on backup, or try it in the demo library.
  • Local network, to reach your NAS on your home network, on a Mac to find the file servers and Apple TVs there, and on Apple TV to pair with PhotoLens on a Mac.
  • Face ID or Touch ID, to unlock Private. Face ID and Touch ID are handled by the system; the app only learns whether it succeeded.
  • Files and folders you choose (Mac), to import the photos you pick and to export originals to the place you pick. The app reads and writes nothing else on your Mac.
  • Background activity, so that backup can continue when the app is not on screen.

You can change these at any time in the Settings app.

Other parties

PhotoLens contains no advertising or analytics software and shares no data with anyone. Three services are involved only because you use them, each under its own terms:

  • Your NAS. You own and control it. What is stored there, and who can reach it, is decided by its settings.
  • Apple iCloud, for the saved NAS connection described above, if iCloud is on for your device.
  • Your VPN provider, for example Tailscale, if you choose to reach your NAS that way. PhotoLens only connects to the address you give it.

If you have agreed to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may show us anonymous crash and usage statistics for the app. They contain no photos and nothing that identifies you.

Keeping and deleting data

  • Photos on your NAS stay there until you delete them. Photos you delete in PhotoLens are moved to a Trash folder on the NAS and removed after 30 days.
  • Settings › Sign Out on All Devices… removes the saved NAS account, with its password, from iCloud and signs your devices out. The address, share name and user name stay on the device, so that you only type the password to sign in again.
  • Deleting the app removes its cache, settings and remembered address from the device.

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to export, correct or delete.

Children

PhotoLens is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, children included.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, this page is updated before the new version is released, and the date at the top changes.
